# Delete your FlashWine account FlashWine is developed by Quentin Sauvage. You can delete your account and data at any time. ## Option 1 — In the app 1. Open FlashWine and sign in. 2. Go to Account → Delete Account. 3. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first. ## Option 2 — Without the app (e.g. uninstalled) Email qsauvage@icloud.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject "FlashWine account deletion". We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email. ## What is deleted - Your account (email, sign-in identifiers, display name) - Your synced study data: progress, XP, streaks, exam attempts, tasting notes and groups, activated study packs ## What is kept - Purchases are held by Google Play (not by us) and are not deleted; they can be restored if you create a new account. - No other data is retained after deletion.
